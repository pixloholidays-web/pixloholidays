How Group Trips Work

1. Pick a departure

Every batch has fixed dates and a fixed per-person price. What you see is what you pay.

2. Reserve on WhatsApp

Tap "Reserve my seat". We confirm availability and share payment details with a GST invoice.

3. Just show up

Tickets, hotels, transfers and sightseeing are handled. We share all trip details on WhatsApp before departure.

Group Trip FAQs

Can I join solo?

Yes, solo travellers are welcome. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll explain the room-sharing options and the single-room price for your batch.

Who else will be on the trip?

Small groups of friends, couples and solo travellers. We keep batches small so the trip still feels personal.

What if I want different dates?

Every group trip is also available as a private package on your own dates — see the full itinerary page, or use the instant price calculator there.

What is the cancellation policy?

Our standard refund policy applies. Any trip-specific terms are shared with you on WhatsApp before you pay.
