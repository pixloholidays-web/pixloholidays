Goa Express Tour: Frequently Asked Questions

Train Travel & Transfers

Which train is used for the Surat to Goa express tour?

We book reserved tickets on the Sampark Kranti Express (Train No. 12450 Ex-Surat and Train No. 12449 Ex-Thivim). You can choose between Sleeper Class (₹4,999) or upgrade to 3 AC Class (₹6,499).

Are station transfers in Goa private or shared?

All station pickup and drop-off transfers between Thivim Railway Station and your hotel are provided in a private AC car for maximum comfort.
