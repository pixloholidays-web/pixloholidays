Complete Himachal Pradesh Travel Guide: Questions & Answers

Best Time to Visit Himachal Pradesh

When is the best time to visit Himachal Pradesh?

The best time to visit Himachal Pradesh is from March to June and September to December. March-June offers pleasant weather (15-25°C) ideal for sightseeing and adventure activities. September-December is perfect for witnessing snow in higher altitudes and enjoying crisp mountain views.

What is Himachal like in winter?

Winter (December-February) transforms Himachal into a snowy wonderland. Shimla, Kufri, and Manali receive snow (1-3 feet), perfect for snow sports. Temperatures range from -5°C to 15°C. Carry heavy woolens, thermals, and waterproof boots.
